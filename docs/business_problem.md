# Business Problem

## Stakeholder
The primary stakeholder is Bellabeat's marketing and product team. The analysis is designed to help stakeholders understand how smart device users behave and how those patterns could inform customer engagement, wellness messaging, and product feature priorities.

## Business Context
Bellabeat is a wellness technology company. To support marketing and product decisions, the company needs to understand how users engage with wearable devices, including daily movement, calories, active minutes, and sleep behaviour.

## Why This Analysis Matters
Smart device usage data can show when users are most active, how consistent their routines are, and whether sleep behaviour differs by user or day type. These patterns can support more relevant marketing campaigns, personalised product messaging, and engagement features.

## Decision Support
This case study can support:

- Marketing segmentation based on activity level.
- Product messaging around goal setting, routine building, and recovery.
- Dashboard reporting for activity and sleep trends.
- Recommendation writing for user engagement campaigns.

## Scope
The analysis uses historical smart device data. It does not include demographic, sales, subscription, or app engagement data, so recommendations should be treated as directional rather than definitive.
